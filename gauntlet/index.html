How to Play

  • Steer — drag anywhere on the left of the screen. The stick appears under your thumb.
  • Boost — tap the right of the screen. Dive to charge the meter, spend it on the straights.
  • Follow the ribbon — the glowing line is the racing line. It dives into the canyons; so should you.
  • Fly the gates — pass them in order. Miss one and the lap will not count.
  • 3 laps, 3 rivals. Clip a rival or the ground and you tumble, so pick your line.
  • Desktop: WASD or arrows to steer, space to boost.
