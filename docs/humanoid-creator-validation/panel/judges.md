# Judge scores (two of three judges completed, 2026-09-03)


## Judge — winner: Humanoid Web — a zero-build Three.js PWA at /humanoid, VRM-first, no Xcode

| Proposal | Feasibility | Headless | End-user | Export risk (10=low) | Owner effort (10=low) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plumb — Linux-Gated Native iPad Humanoid Creator | 6 | 6 | 7 | 6 | 5 | **30** |
| Explicit Rig | 8 | 7 | 7 | 7 | 7 | **36** |
| Kaydara — a pure-Swift FBX 7.4 writer with a Linux-resident correctness loop | 5 | 6 | 7 | 5 | 6 | **29** |
| Humanoid Web — a zero-build Three.js PWA at /humanoid, VRM-first, no Xcode | 7 | 9 | 5 | 7 | 9 | **37** |
| Anvil — Thin iPad, Headless Forge | 6 | 6 | 8 | 6 | 7 | **33** |

**Rationale.**
- *Plumb — Linux-Gated Native iPad Humanoid Creator*: Best-researched proposal and the source of two ideas every other design should steal (Autodesk Linux SDK as the Unity-predictive reader oracle; HumanoidGate as a portable reimplementation of AvatarAutoMapper/AnalyzeIK). But on the owner-effort lens it is the heaviest native plan: three exporter adapters, an Assimp iOS build that still needs the -fembed-bitcode CMake patch verified only on a Mac, a one-time Autodesk EULA-gated download Mentis must place by hand, 6-8 device sessions, and a 'paste Unity verdicts after every session' discipline that puts Mentis back inside the inner loop the design claims to remove. HumanoidGate's leniency failure mode is quiet and only calibrates at M0 day 6. End-user Unity path is 12 steps including Rig tab, Configure and Read/Write — the most friction of the five. FBX oracles (ufbx + Blender's ufbx-backed importer) share ancestry, so the true headless coverage is lower than 70% until the Autodesk reader is wired.
- *Explicit Rig*: The strongest NATIVE design on this lens. A ~1,500-line pure-Swift GLB/VRM writer against a fully normative spec, five independent Linux oracles (Khronos validator at 0 errors AND 0 warnings, VRM JSON schema, real PNG inflate, headless Blender import, CPU LBS oracle), and UniVRM's explicit humanBones map deletes Unity's AvatarAutoMapper from the critical path entirely — no Configure, no T-pose dialog, no forbidden-keyword vetoes. Mentis's Phase-0B is one ~2h session and the plan honestly names the one unverified link (UniVRM-1.0 prefab through the VRChat SDK) and gives a one-day VRM 0.x fallback. Costs: UniVRM is an extra Unity package; MSL still only compiles on a macOS CI runner; the Swift PNG/zlib trap is real but pre-identified. If the web winner's day-3 pen check fails, this is the design to build, and the template forge, corpus, VRM bone table and Unity steps all carry over.
- *Kaydara — a pure-Swift FBX 7.4 writer with a Linux-resident correctness loop*: Intellectually honest and its strict-ufbx / Blender-legacy-importer / structural-diff oracle stack is a genuine improvement over the PRD's lenient reopen. But it deliberately takes on the one task every source in the digest says is 2-4 weeks to first-Unity-green and open-ended after: the undocumented FBX semantic layer that Unity's closed Autodesk importer expects. Its claimed 78% headless is overstated — the semantic layer has no Unity-predictive oracle, and it never uses the Autodesk Linux SDK reader that Plumb identified, which is the single most predictive check available and costs nothing to ship. Its own gate rule ('passes only on the first attempt') concedes the likely outcome is a second Mentis session. The VRM insurance track is the best part and effectively makes this Explicit Rig plus a speculative FBX writer.
- *Humanoid Web — a zero-build Three.js PWA at /humanoid, VRM-first, no Xcode*: On the stated lens this is not close: it removes Xcode, signing, TestFlight, App Store review, the ObjC++/C++-interop bridge, MSL compilation, and the Swift PNG/zlib trap (canvas.toBlob gives a real PNG) in one move. It is the ONLY design where the RENDER layer is headlessly testable — GLSL runs in Playwright's Chromium, so sculpt/paint output can be screenshot-diffed in CI, and the same .js modules run in node --test and in iPad Safari with a byte-identity assertion between browser and Node exports. Mentis's total Phase-0 involvement is ~55 minutes and zero of it is Xcode; per-milestone smoke is a page refresh on Vercel, matching four artefacts this repo already ships this way with the same non-zero-exit harness convention. Verified Safari 18.2 pointer surface (pressure, tilt, coalesced AND predicted events) is better than assumed. The price is end-user quality: touch-to-photon latency through WebKit's compositor is unmeasurable off-device and unfixable if bad, no Pencil Pro squeeze/roll/hover, Safari 7-day storage eviction, no App Store. The proposal gates that risk first and cheapest, which is exactly right.
- *Anvil — Thin iPad, Headless Forge*: Best end-user Unity experience of the five: one package install, then Read/Write, mobile-whitelist shader, ASTC and the Avatar Descriptor are all set before the user sees the prefab — three VRChat requirements an FBX-in-a-ZIP structurally cannot satisfy. The SHA-256 handshake (device export must match CI export) is the cleanest possible device acceptance test and Mentis's first touchpoint is clicking Upload, not opening Xcode. But it trades an untestable iOS FBX writer for a Unity C# ScriptedImporter whose testability rests entirely on Unity 2022.3.22f1 licensing non-interactively in a Linux container — flagged UNKNOWN twice in the research and never settled. If that fails the importer is exactly as Mac-bound as the FBX writer was, plus Mentis now owns a Unity package that churns with every VRChat SDK release. Proposal is admirably blunt about this; the day-1 trip-wire is the right mitigation but the plan's advantage evaporates if it trips.

**Graft into the winner.**
- From Plumb: HumanoidGate / from Kaydara: VRChatRigGate — transcribe Unity's sBonePoses tolerances (UpperArm/LowerArm 5°, Hand 10°, Shoulder 20°, Hips/UpperLeg 15°, LowerLeg/Foot 20°, spine 30°), the 19-bone VRChat SDK 3.10.4 requirement, Chest-not-UpperChest shoulder/neck parentage, ≥0.20 m shoulder height, first-child limb ordering, ≥5 mm inter-joint distance, unique mapped names and forbidden keywords into one JS module (src/core/riggate.js) that runs identically in node --test and in the browser's export blocking checks. Even though VRM bypasses the auto-mapper, VRChat's AnalyzeIK still runs on the built Avatar, so these are live gates.
- From Plumb: tools/corpus/unity-verdicts.json as a first-class CI fixture — after every Mentis Unity session, paste the real per-file verdict; a test fails if the rig gate's prediction ever disagrees. Cheap insurance against the gate being too lenient.
- From Plumb: build the Autodesk FBX SDK Linux distribution (fbx2020310_fbxsdk_gcc_linux, internal dev use under EULA §1.1.1, never committed) as the reader oracle for FBX candidate C — it is the same code Unity's importer uses and is far more predictive than a ufbx or ufbx-backed-Blender reopen.
- From Kaydara: run ufbx with strict=true and disable_quirks=true in the FBX candidate-C corpus, and require Blender's LEGACY Python importer (bpy.ops.import_scene.fbx) as the genuinely independent parser — Blender 5.0+'s default importer is ufbx and shares its blind spots.
- From Anvil: ship an optional HumanoidImportKit.unitypackage whose ScriptedImporter/post-processor sets Mesh Read/Write, a VRChat/Mobile shader (Toon Standard / Standard Lite), ASTC 6x6 texture compression for Android/iOS, and a VRCAvatarDescriptor with ViewPosition from the head bone — and fails import with the NAMED rule rather than an opaque SDK error. Keep UniVRM as the primary path so the kit is upside, not a dependency.
- From Anvil: the Blender companion script (glb_to_fbx.py) with EXPLICIT flags — add_leaf_bones=False, use_armature_deform_only=True, primary_bone_axis='Y', secondary_bone_axis='X', apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_ALL', axis_up='Y', axis_forward='-Z', bake_anim=False — as the FBX escape hatch and as the golden reference for candidate C. Never rely on defaults_unity3d(), which is dead code.
- From Anvil: the day-1 trip-wire — attempt Unity 2022.3.22f1 -batchmode -nographics with a GameCI/UNITY_LICENSE activation on Linux before anything else; if it works, assert Avatar.isValid && isHuman on every corpus .vrm and headless coverage climbs toward ~95%. Report failure honestly on day 2, do not route around it quietly.
- From Explicit Rig: author the template so BOTH VRM 1.0 and VRM 0.x are emittable from day one (T-pose rest, identity local bone rotations, model facing +Z, then flip to -Z for 0.x) so the fallback if the UniVRM-1.0 → VRChat SDK leg fails is a one-day JSON-shape change, not a new exporter.
- From Plumb: paint internally at 2048 but default the export preset to 1024 with downsample on export, and print estimated texture memory for both the compressed (ASTC) and uncompressed brackets in the manifest — texture memory is a ranked mobile stat the PRD omits and the app cannot control Unity's import compression.
- From Kaydara/Plumb: add the two roll poses (forearm pronated 90°, thigh internally rotated) to the pose corpus and preview set; the PRD's four poses contain no roll so candy-wrapper collapse is invisible until Unity. Keep skinning LBS, never DQS, to match Unity's SkinnedMeshRenderer.
- Fallback contract, stated up front: if the day-3 Pencil check fails (latency or pressure unusable), pivot to Explicit Rig. Everything on the Linux side — the frozen template and tables, the golden corpus and negatives, the VRMC_vrm bone table, the validator suite, the Unity/VRChat step list — carries over verbatim; only the writer moves from ~500 lines of JS to ~1,500 lines of Swift plus a hand-rolled RFC-1950 PNG encoder.

**Fatal flaws named.**
- Kaydara (near-fatal on this lens): its entire correctness argument rests on oracles that cannot predict Unity acceptance of the undocumented FBX semantic layer, and it omits the one Linux oracle that can (the Autodesk FBX SDK Linux reader, which Plumb found and which Unity itself uses). The digest's own estimate is 2-4 weeks to first Unity green and open-ended afterwards, with Mentis's Unity session as the only real signal. Its 78% headless claim is not supported by its oracle stack.
- Anvil: a single unresolved unknown (non-interactive Unity 2022.3.22f1 licensing in a Linux container) decides whether its central component — the C# ScriptedImporter — is headlessly testable at all. If it fails, Anvil has replaced an untestable iOS FBX writer with an untestable Unity importer and added a permanent Unity-package maintenance burden that churns with every VRChat SDK release. The proposal names this honestly, but the architecture's advantage over Explicit Rig depends entirely on it.
- Plumb: the design's pitch is 'Mentis out of the inner loop', yet HumanoidGate only becomes trustworthy after Mentis pastes Unity verdicts (M0 day 6) and the calibration discipline requires him after EVERY Unity session; combined with 6-8 device sessions, a Mac-side Unity+SDK install via VPM CLI/ALCOM, hand-placed EULA-gated Autodesk tarballs, and an Assimp iOS build whose bitcode-flag patch can only be verified in Xcode, it is the plan most likely to need iterative Mac-side debugging — the thing this lens punishes. Not fatal, but it undercuts its own thesis.
- Humanoid Web (the winner, but the flaw is real): the product's entire value is pen-driven and WebKit exposes none of Apple's latency levers (event-driven MTKView, maximumDrawableCount=2, presentsWithTransaction, animation suppression); no web-vs-native Pencil latency number exists for a 120 Hz iPad and it cannot be measured on Linux. If the day-3 stroke feels like a web page, the fallback (strength slider, speed-derived falloff) is a visibly worse product and the correct response is to abandon the route, not paper over it. Safari's 7-day storage eviction is a second data-loss hazard that must be mitigated with OPFS autosave plus an explicit Save-to-Files path before any user touches it. No App Store presence if Mentis wants a sellable product.
- Every VRM-route proposal (Explicit Rig, Humanoid Web, Anvil) shares one unverified load-bearing link: no public evidence exists of a VRChat upload from a UniVRM-1.0-imported (or AvatarBuilder-built) Humanoid prefab. Mechanism is verified from source (Vrm10Importer → AvatarBuilder.BuildHumanAvatar; VRChat SDK validates the Unity Avatar, not the file format) but mechanism is not evidence. Each proposal correctly makes this the Phase-0 gate; none should write editor code before it passes.


## Judge — winner: Explicit Rig

| Proposal | Feasibility | Headless | End-user | Export risk (10=low) | Owner effort (10=low) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plumb — Linux-Gated Native iPad Humanoid Creator | 6 | 7 | 8 | 6 | 5 | **32** |
| Explicit Rig | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 7 | **39** |
| Kaydara — a pure-Swift FBX 7.4 writer with a Linux-resident correctness loop | 5 | 7 | 8 | 5 | 6 | **31** |
| Humanoid Web — a zero-build Three.js PWA at /humanoid, VRM-first, no Xcode | 7 | 9 | 5 | 7 | 9 | **37** |
| Anvil — Thin iPad, Headless Forge | 6 | 6 | 8 | 6 | 7 | **33** |

**Rationale.**
- *Plumb — Linux-Gated Native iPad Humanoid Creator*: Risk lens: Plumb is the most research-literate FBX proposal and avoids every REFUTED claim (Autodesk demoted to a Linux READER oracle, Assimp pinned post-#6754 with bJoinIdenticalVertices forced off, ufbx-write with explicit UFBXW_BONE_ROOT). Its Autodesk-Linux-SDK readback is the single most predictive oracle any proposal offers, because Unity's importer is that SDK. But it still rests on the digest's central UNKNOWN — no exporter has EVER been shown to produce a Unity-Humanoid-accepted skinned FBX — and stacks a second unverified model on top: HumanoidGate is a hand-transcription of AvatarAutoMapper/AvatarSetupTool/AnalyzeIK whose lenient-side errors are silent until Mentis reaches Unity (self-admitted). Scope is the largest of the five (three exporter adapters, a C++ gate library, template forge, native Metal app) for a solo owner, and Mentis touchpoints are the heaviest (setup, Phase 0, 6-8 device passes, per-session calibration pastes, two art sessions). Minor factual slip: 'tall' warning quoted at 2.5 m where the SDK source says 5 m. Byte-identical arm64/x86 export is asserted, not proven (FMA contraction can break it).
- *Explicit Rig*: Risk lens: the only proposal whose load-bearing unknowns reduce to ONE inferred link — that a UniVRM-1.0-built Humanoid prefab plus a VRC Avatar Descriptor clears the current VRChat SDK. That link is source-verified on both ends (Vrm10Importer -> AvatarBuilder.BuildHumanAvatar; SDK 3.10.4 AnalyzeIK inspects the Animator's avatar, not the file), isolated in a 2-hour test before any editor code, and has a one-day fallback (VRM 0.x) that only works because the template is designed for both profiles from day one. Everything else is executed-and-verified: Swift 6.3.3 on Ubuntu with XCTest+Swift Testing, Swift/C++ interop against real ufbx.c and tiny_bvh.h, a 54-line skinned GLB passing Khronos 0/0/0, gltf-validator from npm, Blender 5.2.1 headless, XcodeGen on Linux. It correctly absorbs the REFUTED items (raw-DEFLATE PNG trap -> own PNGCodec + inflate test; ObjC++ bridge -> .Cxx interop; Foundation-only core; macOS VCC GUI does not exist). It deletes Unity's AvatarAutoMapper from the critical path, which is where most catalogued FBX failure strings live. Costs: one extra MIT package install (honestly stated), a native Metal/Pencil app is still real work, MSL compiles only on a macOS runner. UniVRM v0.131.2 against 2022.3.22f1 specifically is unconfirmed and must be pinned in Phase-0B.
- *Kaydara — a pure-Swift FBX 7.4 writer with a Linux-resident correctness loop*: Risk lens: Kaydara chooses the least-evidenced export path of all five — a from-scratch FBX semantic layer — against the digest's explicit verdict that this is 'NOT a reliable 1-2 week task' and 'realistically 2-4 weeks to first-Unity-green, open-ended for finger/elbow-pose correctness', and against the PRD's own rule. It budgets 8 days (M1 3 + M2 5). Its oracle analysis contains a real error: it states the Autodesk FBX SDK 'is what Unity actually uses and what we cannot run on Linux' — but the digest CONFIRMS a Linux distribution (fbx2020310_fbxsdk_gcc_linux.tar.gz), so Kaydara forgoes the strongest available oracle while correctly noting that ufbx and Blender's default importer share a ufbx blind spot. Its strengths are genuine: strict/disable_quirks ufbx, Blender legacy Python importer as an independent parser, structural diff against Blender golden output with explicit flags (correctly noting defaults_unity3d() is dead code), the VRM insurance track in week two, and a rigorous VRChatRigGate. The 'first attempt or fail' gate rule is honest. No REFUTED claim is load-bearing, but the whole plan rests on the same UNKNOWN as every FBX route with even less prior mileage than Assimp.
- *Humanoid Web — a zero-build Three.js PWA at /humanoid, VRM-first, no Xcode*: Risk lens: the most likely of the five to ship SOMETHING, and by far the best on the two constraints Mentis actually stated (90% headless, page-refresh validation, reuses the repo's proven Playwright harness and Share-sheet code). Same VRM/UniVRM unknown as Explicit Rig, same cheap gate. But it rests on an UNKNOWN that is unmeasurable off-device and unfixable if wrong: Apple Pencil touch-to-photon latency through WebKit's compositor versus MTKView with presentsWithTransaction/double-buffering — the proposal itself says failure means 'reconsider the native route'. Pen DATA availability (Safari 18.2 coalesced/predicted/altitude/azimuth) is credible, but no Pencil Pro squeeze/roll/hover, no estimated-property updates. It also abandons the PRD's deliverable (native SwiftUI/Metal iPad app, App Store) for a PWA with Safari 7-day storage eviction, tab discard under memory pressure, and iOS Safari memory ceilings for a 2048^2 render-target paint pipeline plus Three. Every shipping iPad sculpt tool in the digest (Nomad, Valence, Satin) is native GPU code, though Nomad's WASM web build shows browser sculpting is not unprecedented. Comfy FBX in Node is correctly non-blocking. Scored down on quality, not on shipping probability.
- *Anvil — Thin iPad, Headless Forge*: Risk lens: Anvil makes an UNKNOWN load-bearing by design — Unity 2022.3.22f1 batchmode on Linux with non-interactive licensing — and admits that if it fails, its bespoke C# ScriptedImporter becomes exactly as untestable as the FBX writer it replaced, having also consumed a milestone. The fallback (GameCI/macos-26 runner) is plausible but the proposal itself calls the trip-wire 'non-negotiable'. It trades UniVRM (MIT, maintained, source-verified Avatar building) for a second codebase Mentis must own and only Unity can test. One technical claim is wrong as written: a texture created inside a ScriptedImporter is a sub-asset with no TextureImporter, so 'sets the texture importer to ASTC 6x6' needs a sidecar PNG or EditorUtility.CompressTexture — fixable, but it dents the 'importer sets all three' argument. Genuinely strong ideas: the SHA-256 hash handshake between CI and device export, Unity-side enforcement of Mesh Read/Write + mobile shader + descriptor, named VRChat preflight rules from SDK source, Blender companion with explicit flags as FBX escape hatch, miniz for cross-platform determinism, and the correct observation that a desktop was never optional. End-user quality would be the best of the five IF the importer ships. Same VRM/VRChat inferred link as Explicit Rig, plus one more untested component in front of it.

**Graft into the winner.**
- From Anvil: the SHA-256 hash handshake — the .vrm the physical iPad exports must byte-match the .vrm CI produced from the same document. Requires a hand-rolled deterministic JSON writer with fixed key order (JSONSerialization key order differs between Apple Foundation and swift-corelibs-foundation), floats only in the binary buffer, and a pinned compressor (vendored miniz beats system zlib, whose version differs per platform). Turns Mentis's device export check into a one-line diff.
- From Anvil + Plumb (HumanoidImportKit): ship a tiny OPTIONAL Unity editor script alongside the .vrm (not a full importer) that runs after UniVRM import — asserts avatar.isHuman, sets Mesh Read/Write, swaps the material to VRChat/Mobile/Toon Standard, sets the albedo's Android/iOS platform override to ASTC and Max Size 1024, adds VRCAvatarDescriptor with ViewPosition from the head bone, and prints named VRChat preflight failures (19 bones, Chest parentage, >=0.20 m shoulder, first-child limb ordering). Keeps UniVRM as the Avatar builder, removes Anvil's untestable-importer risk, and closes the three mobile gates an FBX-in-a-ZIP can never set.
- From Plumb: the unity-verdicts.json calibration fixture — every Mentis Unity session records the real per-corpus-file verdict, and CI fails if the app's export-time VRChat gate ever disagrees with a recorded verdict. Explicit Rig has the gate but not the calibration loop.
- From Plumb: paint internally at 2048 and downsample to a 1024 export default (2048 opt-in), and print estimated texture memory for BOTH the ASTC-compressed and uncompressed brackets in the manifest. VRChat's Android guidance caps at 1024, the Android bundle has a hard 10 MB cap, and texture memory is a ranked stat the PRD omits.
- From Plumb and Kaydara: add two roll poses to the preview/golden set — forearm pronated 90 degrees and thigh internally rotated. Without them candy-wrapper collapse in linear-blend skinning is invisible until Unity. Also inspect per-bone weights rather than parent_set's return value (bone-heat fails silently with {'FINISHED'}) and renormalise (Blender output sums 0.976-0.994).
- From Anvil: the Blender companion script (blender --background --python glb_to_fbx.py) as the FBX escape hatch and structural oracle, with flags set EXPLICITLY — add_leaf_bones=False, use_armature_deform_only=True, primary/secondary_bone_axis 'Y'/'X', apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_ALL', axis_up 'Y', axis_forward '-Z', bake_anim=False — never inherited (defaults_unity3d() is uncalled dead code, and Unity's own .blend path inherits leaf bones ON). Gives users who need FBX for Blender/Unreal a proven path without the app writing FBX.
- From Humanoid Web and Plumb: rasterise the paint brush in UV space (vertex shader emits uv*2-1 as clip position, fragment evaluates the footprint from the world-space Pencil hit) so dabs crossing seams land on every island by construction; keep the PRD's precomputed seam-partner map as the TEST oracle and cheap fallback rather than the mechanism. Web's dab-descriptor undo log replayed from RT checkpoints is cheaper on mobile than tile readback.
- From Kaydara: the 'gate passes only if the Unity session is green on the FIRST attempt' rule — a second Mentis session needed to reach green is itself the signal that the Linux oracle stack has a blind spot, and the response is to close the blind spot before spending another of his sessions.
- From Anvil and Web: run the Unity-2022.3.22f1-on-Linux batchmode spike (Personal license via GameCI-style activation, or a macos-26 runner) on day 1 as UPSIDE, never as plan; if it lands, Avatar.isValid/isHuman and the HumanDescription dump move into CI and Mentis's Phase-0B becomes pure confirmation.
- From Plumb: in the Unity onboarding, state that Windows + Android is the minimum for cross-platform visibility (iOS falls back to the Android build with shader replacement), and that since VRChat 2025.3.1 the rank players see is server-computed and may differ from the SDK panel — the app reports counts, never a rank.

**Fatal flaws named.**
- Kaydara: budgets ~8 agent-days (M1 + M2) for a from-scratch FBX semantic layer that the research digest explicitly rates at 2-4 weeks to first-Unity-green and 'open-ended' for pose correctness, against the PRD's own 'do not begin a custom FBX writer' rule. Worse, its weakest-point analysis asserts the Autodesk FBX SDK 'cannot run on Linux' — the digest CONFIRMS a Linux distribution exists — so the plan forgoes the one oracle (Unity's actual importer library) that would most reduce its central risk. If the spike fails, the plan has replaced an untestable iOS FBX writer with an untestable Unity importer after spending a milestone, and Mentis inherits a Unity package to maintain instead of using UniVRM. Its 'importer sets ASTC via the texture importer' claim is also wrong as stated for a ScriptedImporter sub-asset (no TextureImporter exists; needs a sidecar PNG or EditorUtility.CompressTexture).
- Humanoid Web: rests on an UNKNOWN that is unmeasurable off-device and unfixable if wrong — Apple Pencil touch-to-photon latency through WebKit versus a native MTKView with presentsWithTransaction and a 2-drawable buffer ring — and abandons the PRD's stated deliverable (native SwiftUI/Metal iPad app, App Store) for a PWA subject to Safari's 7-day storage eviction, background tab discard, and iOS Safari memory ceilings for a 2048^2 render-target paint pipeline plus Three. Byte-identical arm64/x86 export is asserted, not proven (FMA contraction), and the 'tall' warning band is misquoted (2.5 m vs the SDK's 5 m).

If both VRM profiles fail, the plan reverts to the PRD's FBX gate having spent three agent-days and two of Mentis's hours — an acceptable, bounded loss.
