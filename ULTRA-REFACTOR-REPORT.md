## TL;DR verdict

**Birb holds ~60fps on a mid-range phone today.** The headline "performance crisis" is largely overstated, because the genuinely expensive things are *already* mobile-gated: the transparent double-sided canopy ceilings are **never constructed on mobile**, `cloudShell` is null on mobile, antialias is desktop-only, DPR is capped at a conservative 1.2, prop counts are mobile-reduced, and an inline FPS-tier adaptive-quality controller is wired and running.

Adversarial verification **refuted or downgraded the top steady-state perf findings** and left exactly **one** confirmed mobile frame-risk: the **drone explosion**. Every kill spawns ~60 additive transparent meshes + 60 materials + ~70 `Vector3` in-frame, not mobile-gated — and it is **fully redundant**, because the same kill already fires two cheap 1-draw-call Points bursts. In Drone Hunter, 2–3 overlapping 1.2s explosions stack ~120–180 extra additive draws + a GC burst exactly when the budget is tightest. **The single highest-leverage perf fix is a deletion.**

The most valuable *non-perf* findings are player-facing and code-health: a **dead glide-speed slider** (moves a % readout but never changes cruise), **auto-leveling that fights deliberate climbs every frame**, a **Boost button overlapping the flight joystick spawn zone**, and **~3,986 lines of dead/unwired code** (including the entire `src/performance/` engine the game reimplements inline) in a repo whose product value *is* readability.

## Reconciliation corrections (orchestrator, post-verification)

The raw force-ranking and the verified reality diverge at the top of the Performance list. Corrections applied to this report:

- **Perf #1 "6-light full-screen fill-rate killer" → demoted to minor code-health.** Verified false on mechanism: the world/ground is `MeshLambertMaterial` + `flatShading` (confirmed — there is *no* `MeshStandardMaterial` anywhere in `spherical-world.js`), so lighting is **per-vertex Gouraud over ~12k verts, not per-fragment over the viewport**. Dropping 6→3 lights saves <1fps. The fix is harmless but not a lever. The code comment at `spherical-world.js:2184` claiming "per-fragment" is itself wrong and should be corrected.
- **Perf #5 "canopy-ceiling overdraw" → refuted on the mobile target.** The meshes are short-circuited by `_isMobile` (`spherical-world.js:794` / `:1703`) and never exist on a phone. Real cost is desktop-only; the `DoubleSide`→`FrontSide` flip is still a free correctness win.
- **Perf #4 collision scan (~0.02ms) and #6 minimap (~0.2–0.6ms) → code-health, not frame-budget threats.** Real but sub-millisecond on the target device; the minimap throttle is the only one worth doing for milliseconds.
- **Perf #2 drone explosion → confirmed and *strengthened*.** Verification found the redundant Points bursts already fire on the same kill (`index.html:6925` + `:6929`), so the fix is a pure deletion and the "120 Vector3" figure is ~70. **Promoted to global #1.**
- My own independent reads corroborate the per-frame micro-allocations: `getAmbientOffsets` fallback allocates a `Vector3`+`Quaternion` every frame because `BirdFlight` has no such method (the ternary else-branch is always taken), and the bird model is re-traversed via `getObjectByName` every frame.

## Priority dimension: does Birb hold 60fps on a mid phone?

**Verdict.** Yes — Birb almost certainly holds ~60fps on a mid phone (iPhone 12+, Android Chrome) TODAY, and the headline "performance crisis" findings are largely overstated. The make-or-break dimension is in better shape than the raw ranked list implies, because the dev has already mobile-gated the genuinely expensive things: the two transparent DoubleSide canopy-ceiling InstancedMeshes are never even constructed on mobile (spherical-world.js:794/:1703 short-circuit on _isMobile), cloudShell is null on mobile, antialias is desktop-only, DPR is capped at a conservative 1.2 (not the documented 1.4), structural prop counts are mobile-reduced, and an inline FPS-sampling adaptive-quality tier controller is wired and running. Adversarial verification REFUTED the #1 ranked finding (the "6-light full-screen ground sphere fill-rate killer"): the ground is MeshLambertMaterial with flatShading, so lighting is per-VERTEX Gouraud over ~12k verts, not per-fragment over the viewport — dropping lights saves <1fps and is not a fill-rate lever. The #5 ranked canopy-overdraw finding was REFUTED outright on mobile (mesh doesn't exist there). The #4 collision-scan and #6 minimap findings were confirmed real but measured at ~0.02ms and ~0.2-0.6ms/frame respectively — code-health, not frame-budget threats.
